# Micro SaaS Examples 2026: Real Companies + Revenue

As of October 2026, micro SaaS is a skewed business. Across 8,000+ startups tracked in BigIdeasDB, the **3,787 with active revenue** average **$4,298 MRR**, the median is just **$145 MRR**, and only **6.1%** clear $10K MRR. The ceiling is far higher: eight verified micro SaaS companies make **$1M to $8M a year**, led by one company with 13 people and no investors. This guide pairs those named companies with category benchmarks and 25 buildable blueprints.

The numbers here come from [BigIdeasDB's Revenue Intelligence](https://bigideasdb.com/help/revenue-intelligence-tool), where we track **8,000+ SaaS startups** — of which **3,787 report active revenue**. Among those earning, the average is **$4,298 MRR**, but the median is just **$145 MRR**, and only about **6.1% clear $10K MRR**. That gap is the real story of micro SaaS: most stay tiny, a small share scale hard, and the winners below show the top of that distribution.

### How much revenue do micro SaaS companies make?

Based on 8,000+ startups tracked in BigIdeasDB (3,787 with active revenue), the average is $4,298 MRR and the median is $145. Only about 6.1% clear $10K MRR. The verified ceiling is much higher: Submagic makes $8M a year with 13 people and no investors, Plausible around $3.5M a year, Rezi around $200K a month, and Tally around $150K a month. Most micro SaaS stays small and a handful scale hard, and both facts are true at the same time.

### What are the most profitable micro SaaS categories?

By profit margin, the strongest categories in our data are Games (83.6%), Mobile Apps (78.2%), Community tools (77.3%), and Developer Tools (74.0% across 533 startups). Developer Tools and AI (1,955 startups at 272% average 30-day growth) pair high margins with strong demand.

### Can one person run a micro SaaS?

Yes. Three of the eight verified examples above are run by a single founder: Bannerbear (Jon Yongfook, ~$1M a year), Testimonial.to (Damon Chen, $1M+ a year), and Photo AI (Pieter Levels, ~$130K a month with zero employees). Solo founders focus on a single feature, automate support, and keep costs low. See our [micro SaaS ideas guide](https://bigideasdb.com/help/micro-saas-ideas-guide) for a step-by-step breakdown.

## What You’ll Discover

## 20 Remote Jobs That Pay Well

High-paying [remote jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs) span industries, with top roles like cloud architect, data scientist, medical director, and marketing director offering strong salaries and long-term growth. While many of the highest-paying remote jobs are senior-level positions that require education or experience, roles at all career stages can still offer competitive pay and advancement potential.

The list below highlights 20 remote jobs that pay well, with salary ranges from [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/), and examples of roles that can lead to six-figure earnings as you gain experience and specialized skills.

### 1\. [Actuary Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/actuary)

Salary Range: $62,000–$170,000

Using mathematics, statistics, and financial theory, actuaries assess risk for businesses, particularly in finance and insurance. These top-paying remote jobs often require strong analytical skills, proficiency in actuarial software, and professional certification from organizations such as the Society of Actuaries (SOA) or the Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS).

### 2. [Art Director Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/art-director)

Salary Range: $56,000–$122,000

Art directors oversee the visual style and tone for magazines, newspapers, websites, product packaging, and more. Common duties for this top-paying remote job include developing visual direction, selecting design elements, managing graphic designers and other staff, and presenting designs to clients. Art directors usually need a bachelor’s degree in art or a related area and previous work experience.

### 3. [Business Development Manager Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/business-development)

Salary Range: $54,000–$141,000

Business development managers develop business plans, identify new client sources, and manage accounts to meet sales goals. Ultimately, this high-paying remote job requires the ability to increase revenue. Sales and selling skills are essential to business development, as are client relations skills to maintain business relationships.

### 4. [Clinical Trial Manager Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/clinical-trial-manager)

Salary Range: $76,000–$157,000

Clinical trial managers recruit patients for trials, ensure trials run smoothly and comply with safety standards, review results, update trial structures, and more. Remote, high-paying jobs in clinical trial management require strong medical knowledge, relevant education, and a background in clinical research.

### 5. [Cloud Architect Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/search?search=Cloud+Architect)

Salary Range: $86,000–$183,000

Cloud architects oversee companies’ cloud computing strategies. This top-paying remote job involves designing cloud applications, managing and monitoring them, and developing adoption plans. Cloud architects also need knowledge of operating systems, networking, programming languages, and cloud security.

### 6. [Data Architect Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/data-architect)

Salary Range: $86,000–$181,000

Data architects design, structure, and maintain large-scale databases and data systems that organizations rely on for analytics and operations. This high-paying remote role typically requires expertise in database management, cloud platforms, and data modeling tools, such as structured query language (SQL), non-relational databases (NoSQL), and extract, transform, load (ETL) frameworks.

### 7. [Data Scientist Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/data-scientist)

Salary Range: $73,000–$146,000

Data scientists use programming languages, like Python, R, and SQL, along with machine learning algorithms and statistical techniques, to analyze large datasets, extract insights, build predictive models, and help businesses make data-driven decisions. This high-paying remote job requires strong analytical skills, experience with data visualization tools, and, often, a degree in mathematics, statistics, or computer science.

### 8. [DevOps Engineer Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/devops-engineer)

Salary Range: $78,000–$165,000

Development and operations (DevOps) engineers streamline and automate software development and IT operations, helping teams deploy updates faster and more reliably. These stay-at-home jobs that pay well require experience with continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) tools, cloud infrastructure, and scripting languages like Python, Bash, or Ruby.

### 9. [Finance Director Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/finance-director)

Salary Range: $75,000–$180,000

High-paying remote finance director jobs involve managing accounting and financial control functions, establishing financial strategy, overseeing finance departments, conducting assessments, generating reports, and establishing budgets. A master’s degree in business administration, finance, or economics may be required to land this role.

### 10. [Front-End Developer Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/front-end-developer)

Salary Range: $60,000–$133,000

Front-end developers build HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for websites and web apps to ensure accurate visuals and user-friendly navigation. These well-paying remote jobs sometimes require back-end or visual design experience, such as SQL, Java, PHP, Ruby, or .NET.

### 11\. [Lawyer Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/lawyer)

Salary Range: $68,000–$189,000

Lawyers provide legal advice, draft contracts, represent clients in legal matters, and ensure compliance with laws and regulations. Remote lawyers provide consultations and legal services virtually and may earn higher pay by specializing in areas such as corporate law, intellectual property, employment law, or contract law. They must earn a Juris Doctor (JD) degree and pass the bar exam.

### 12\. [Marketing Director Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/marketing-director)

Salary Range: $62,000–$170,000

A marketing director leads a marketing department by strategizing and implementing marketing campaigns. These good-paying remote jobs involve management, research, and trend analysis to develop marketing strategies. A bachelor’s degree and multiple years of experience are typically required for a marketing director role, especially if you’re seeking top pay.

### 13\. [Medical Director Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/medical-director)

Salary Range: $94,000–$300,000

One of the highest-paying remote jobs, this physician-level role oversees a healthcare organization’s operations. Medical directors create protocols and guidelines, lead clinical staff, oversee quality assurance, and liaise with network facilities. A Doctor of Medicine (MD) or a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (DO) degree is typically required.

### 14. [Medical Writer Jobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/remote-jobs/medical-writer)

Salary Range: $69,000–$118,000

Medical writers turn technical and scientific data from studies and research into papers, articles, and documents that present information clearly. Knowledge of medical terms and processes is required, and certification with the American Medical Writers Association (AMWA) can be a bonus and may boost your income.
